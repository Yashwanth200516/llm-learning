Once, in a small village surrounded by green hills,
there lived a boy named Arjun who loved exploring the
forest near his home. One evening, while walking through
the trees, he found a tiny wooden box hidden beneath a rock.
Inside was an old compass that always pointed toward something 
other than north.

Curious, Arjun followed the compass deep into the forest. 
After walking for hours, he discovered a small waterfall with 
a cave behind it. Inside the cave was a wall covered with glowing 
drawings of the village. He realized they told the story of an 
ancient tree that protected the village from storms.

Arjun returned home and told the villagers what he had found. 
Together, they searched for the tree and discovered it standing 
on a hill overlooking the village. From that day onward, the 
villagers cared for the tree and the forest.

Years later, Arjun became known as the village's greatest 
explorer, but he always said his greatest discovery was learning 
to respect and protect nature.
